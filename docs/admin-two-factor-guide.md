# Administrator sign-in with two-factor authentication

## First setup

1. Sign in through the Staff portal with your phone number and password. Complete a required password change if prompted.
2. Choose Set up authenticator. Open an authenticator app you trust on your phone and scan the QR code. On the same phone, use Cannot scan? Enter a setup key and enter the private key into your app as a time-based account.
3. Enter the current six-digit code and choose Verify and continue.
4. Keep access to the authenticator app. Future password sign-ins also require its current code.

This applies to Main Admin and campus admins, including accounts with an admin membership while viewing another role. Other accounts keep their existing sign-in flow. Verification does not expand your campus or permissions.

## Codes and connection problems

Codes change regularly. If a code is rejected, use the latest code and ensure your phone's date/time is automatic. If the connection fails, retry; do not share your code with another person. A timed-out initial enrolment can leave an unverified setup: reload, then start setup again. Only abandoned unverified factors belonging to this portal setup are removed.

Keep your QR code and setup key private. They can generate future sign-in codes. Do not send them to school staff, support chats or screenshots. Use Sign out when leaving a shared device.

## Lost or replaced phone

Contact the school's authorised system owner. They must verify your identity and use authorised Supabase account-factor recovery. Ordinary staff password resets cannot remove an authenticator, and campus admins cannot reset another admin's factor through this portal. After operator recovery, sign in and enrol your replacement authenticator. Never disable MFA globally just to reset one user's factor.

Real-device and existing-account recovery acceptance must be signed off with the school owner. This guide does not claim that recovery has already been rehearsed for the school's real administrators.
